Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

ClipForge is a private tool that its owner runs to publish short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what ClipForge does with Google account data.

Data we access

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, ClipForge asks Google for two permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload videos to that channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's name and the view, like and comment counts of videos ClipForge uploaded.
  • See, edit and permanently delete your YouTube videos (youtube.force-ssl): once an hour ClipForge checks the status of every video on the channel and deletes any video YouTube has rejected, blocked in a country, age-restricted or flagged for a copyright or policy problem. It never edits or deletes anything else.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports for your YouTube content (yt-analytics.readonly): to show the channel owner their own statistics in the ClipForge dashboard (views, watch time, audience age, gender and country, traffic sources and devices). These reports are only read and shown to the channel owner; they are never shared or sold.
  • Instagram (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish, instagram_business_manage_insights): to publish Reels to the owner's own Instagram professional account and read their follower count and Reel view counts for the dashboard. Nothing else is posted, read or shared.

How we use it

  • Uploading videos to the connected channel, with titles, descriptions and tags.
  • Reading view counts of those uploads to learn which videos perform best.

ClipForge does not read, change or delete any other content, and does not access email, contacts or any other Google service.

Storage and sharing

  • Login tokens are stored encrypted, only on the owner's own computer or private server.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • ClipForge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions. Removing access stops all uploads, and the stored token is deleted when the channel is disconnected in ClipForge.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's issue page.
