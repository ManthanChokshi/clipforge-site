ClipForge

A private tool that turns gaming live-stream highlights into short vertical videos for our own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Watches public live streams from creators who gave permission to be clipped.
  • Finds exciting moments, edits them into Shorts with captions, and rates them.
  • Uploads the best clips to the YouTube channels we own, using the official YouTube Data API.

Who can use it

ClipForge is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.
